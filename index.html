How scoring works

Level 1Newcomer0 XPLevel 6Advanced amateur500 XP
Level 2Dabbler20 XPLevel 7Semi-pro1,000 XP
Level 3Beginner50 XPLevel 8Professional2,500 XP
Level 4Hobbyist100 XPLevel 9Veteran5,000 XP
Level 5Committed amateur250 XPLevel 10Master10,000 XP

Only active practice counts. Passive time, like watching, reading about a skill, or sitting through a lecture, earns nothing.

One session can build more than one skill. Split the time between them, or give a skill part of the time when it was only part of the work.

Rust: after a full year without practice, a skill's effective level drops by 10% of its XP per extra year, capped at 30%. Lifetime XP never disappears.
